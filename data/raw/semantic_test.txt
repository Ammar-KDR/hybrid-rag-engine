Kubernetes Pods are the smallest deployable computing units in Kubernetes. A Pod can contain one or more containers.

Containers inside the same Pod share networking and storage resources. They are scheduled together on the same node.

Readiness probes determine whether a container is ready to accept traffic.

When a readiness probe fails, Kubernetes removes the Pod from matching Service endpoints until the probe succeeds again.

PersistentVolumes provide storage resources in a Kubernetes cluster.

A PersistentVolumeClaim allows a user to request storage without needing to understand the underlying storage implementation.

StorageClasses describe different classes of storage offered by the cluster.
